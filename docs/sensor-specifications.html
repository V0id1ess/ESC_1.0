RTRD Sensor Specifications

Detailed specifications for the onboard sensors used in the real-time recording drone.

Environmental Sensors

  • Temperature: -40°C to 85°C, ±0.5°C accuracy
  • Humidity: 0%-100%, ±2% RH accuracy
  • Barometric Pressure: 300-1100 hPa, ±1 hPa accuracy

Navigation & Positioning

  • GPS Receiver: multi-band support, position accuracy < 2.5 m
  • Altitude Sensor: barometric and ultrasonic altitude sensing
  • IMU: 9-axis inertial measurement unit with gyro/accel/magnetometer

Imaging & Data Capture

  • FPV Camera: 1080p video streaming at 60 fps
  • Data Logger: microSD storage with FAT32 support
  • Telemetry: 2.4 GHz wireless transmit/receive at up to 1 km range

Compliance & Ratings

Designed for robust field operation with shock-resistant housings and electromagnetic compatibility. Meets common UAV safety and environmental standards.
