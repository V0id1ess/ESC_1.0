Getting Started

Before your first flight, verify the drone is fully charged and all sensors are calibrated. Confirm the remote control, ground station, and data link are functioning.

Pre-Flight Checklist

  • Inspect the frame and propellers for damage.
  • Confirm battery voltage and secure the battery.
  • Verify GPS lock and compass calibration.
  • Check sensor readiness in the app dashboard.
  • Confirm the return-to-home altitude is set appropriately.

Operating the Drone

  1. Power on the drone and wait for initialization.
  2. Connect via the primary control app or ground station software.
  3. Select an autonomous flight plan or manual control mode.
  4. Monitor live sensor telemetry and GPS status continuously.
  5. Use the land command when the mission is complete or if an alert appears.

Post-Flight Procedure

After landing, power down safely, remove the battery, and offload recorded data for review. Inspect the aircraft and maintain firmware updates regularly.
